# Research Protocol

研究负责提供可核查的事实，写作负责把这些事实组织成有压力、有转折、有后果的阅读路径。不要把搜索结果摘要当作已打开的来源。

## Scope and source rules

Start from the current runtime topic and define the object, period, place, and disputed terms before searching. If several interpretations are plausible, use the one closest to the user's wording that has the strongest evidence. Put any material scope choice, excluded interpretation, and unresolved ambiguity at the top of `知识库.md`.

Prefer primary records and first-party documents, then reputable reporting, archives, scholarly work, and verifiable statements from involved parties. Open the actual page or document for every consequential source.

- Give every source a stable ID such as `[S01]`.
- Put source IDs beside the claims they support and reuse the same ID for the same source.
- Verify central and high-impact claims with two genuinely independent sources when possible. Reports that repeat one wire story, or pages within one organization, are not independent confirmation.
- Quote wording exactly. Preserve the source language and mark any translation.
- Label consequential claims as `事实`, `当事方说法`, `媒体判断`, or `作者推断`.
- Record the verification date and the period described by changing figures, roles, and statuses.
- Treat a dated document as evidence only for what it showed at that date.
- Record conflicting accounts, inaccessible evidence, and uncertainty instead of silently resolving them.

After the initial research, run one gap-filling pass and one contradiction pass over the unresolved central claims. Stop when those passes produce no material new fact, or when the remaining uncertainty has been documented. Before drafting, reopen the sources supporting the article's central claims.

## Knowledge base

`知识库.md` must contain these sections, adapting names when a section has no literal equivalent:

1. Research scope and ambiguity
2. Background
3. Core people, organizations, or other central actors
4. Timeline
5. Turning points
6. Key numbers, with definitions and comparison caveats
7. Representative works, products, or events
8. Controversies and competing accounts
9. Common misunderstandings
10. Impact
11. Evidence boundaries and remaining uncertainty
12. Source index
13. 终稿事实核对

The source index must list every source's title, institution or publisher, URL, source type, actual access date in this run, and what it supports.

## Source-to-prose handoff

Research establishes what is true; it does not dictate the article's voice. Before drafting, use the knowledge base to identify:

- the real person, users, institution, product, place, or decision that carries the consequences;
- the one question the article will keep pursuing;
- the plain-language meaning of each consequential law, notice, statement, or number;
- the qualifications, exceptions, and conflicts that must survive translation; and
- the minimum exact wording, if any, that must be quoted because paraphrase would change its meaning.

Do not draft by stitching together official sentences, report paragraphs, or media commentary. Prefer author-written, accurate paraphrase. Keep attribution where it is materially part of the event or dispute, rather than adding authority phrases as decoration. When exact wording is legally or logically decisive, quote only the necessary span and explain it immediately in ordinary language.

## Final fact ledger

The final article and compact article must remain free of source IDs, but every consequential statement must be traceable through the knowledge base. After the prose is final, complete the `终稿事实核对` section with one row per consequential claim:

| 文章 | 位置或短引文 | 主张类型 | 来源 ID | 支撑内容 | 核查结果 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 终稿 | 具体小节与可定位短句 | 事实/当事方说法/媒体判断/作者推断 | `[S01]` | 原文数字、事实或推理依据 | 已核对/需降级/待补 |

Use a separate row or a clearly marked compact row for every compact claim that changes the reader's understanding. A compact statement can point to the approved long-form passage as well as its underlying source IDs. Do not leave a `待补` row when reporting `完成`; revise or remove the affected article statement instead.

If source access is unavailable or the central claims cannot be supported without fabrication, stop the dependent writing and report the precise blocker. Do not invent citations, quotations, URLs, or findings.
